# Shop Tracker – How to use

## 1. Put it on your phone (one time)
Open the link you were given and log in with the password.

- **Android (Chrome):** tap the green **Install** button at the top of the app
  (also in **Settings → App on your phone**). If you don't see it, tap **⋮** (top right) → **Install app**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** tap **Share** (square with arrow) → **Add to Home Screen**.

A green basket icon called **Shop** appears on your home screen. Open it from there from now on.
You stay logged in for 30 days.

## 2. Every day (Today tab)
1. **Sale:** type the day's **Cash** and **Card** sale, tap **Save sale**.
2. **Expenses:** tap **+ Add expense** → tap the category → type the amount → **Add expense**.
   Choose Cash / Card / Bank if it wasn't paid in cash. A note is optional.
3. **Other income:** money that is not a sale (refund, owner puts cash in) → **+ Add income**.

The green card at the top shows the day's **Profit**. It turns red and says **Loss** if expenses are more than money in.
Tap **Show breakdown** to see every cost behind it.

- **Wrong entry?** Tap the expense to change it, or tap the bin to delete it.
- **Forgot a day?** Use the **‹ ›** arrows or tap the date to go to any day.

## 3. Fixed costs (Settings tab)
Rent, salary, visa, licence and so on are set **once** here, not typed every day.

- **Add every day:** the app adds a share to each day automatically.
  Example: rent 18,000 per year adds about 49.32 to every day.
- **I enter when paid:** nothing is added automatically. Add it as an expense on the day you pay.

If an amount changes (new rent, new salary), tap the cost and edit it. To stop one, untick **Active**,
or set an **Until** date so old reports stay correct.

## 4. Credits tab
- **Customers:** people who buy now and pay later.
  Tap the name → **Gave credit** when they take goods, **Received payment** when they pay.
- **Suppliers:** **Bought on credit** when you take stock without paying, **Paid** when you pay them.
- **+ New** adds a person. The number next to each name is what is owed right now.

## 5. Reports and Excel (Reports tab)
Tap **This Week**, **This Month**, **Last Month**, or **Custom** to pick any dates, then **Download Excel**.
The Excel file has 4 sheets: **Summary**, **Daily** (one row per day), **Expenses**, **Credits**.

## 6. Backup (once a month)
**Settings → Download full backup** saves everything ever entered as one Excel file.
Send it to yourself on WhatsApp or email and keep it.

## Problems?
- **"Wrong password"** – check with the person who set up the app.
- **Page doesn't load** – check the internet connection and open the app again.
